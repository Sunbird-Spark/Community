# Spark demos and Sandbox

Short walkthroughs of the three roles a course passes through: the <mark style="color:blue;">**Content Creator**</mark> who builds it, the <mark style="color:orange;">**Content Reviewer**</mark> who reviews it, and the <mark style="color:green;">**Learner**</mark> who takes it.

## Build a course

A <mark style="color:blue;">**Content Creator**</mark> signs in, creates a course, fills in its details, adds a lesson unit and a profile, and submits it for review. After the course is approved, they come back to create a batch so learners can enroll, and set up the certificate learners receive on completion.

## Review and publish

Before any course reaches learners, a <mark style="color:orange;">**Content Reviewer**</mark> checks it. This keeps quality consistent across everything published on Sunbird Spark.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open the review queue

The reviewer signs in and opens the courses that have been submitted for review.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check the course

They go through the course details and content to make sure everything is complete and correct.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Approve or send back

If it is ready, they <mark style="color:green;">**approve**</mark> it and the course is published. If something needs fixing, they <mark style="color:red;">**send it back**</mark> to the creator with comments.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open to learners

Once the course is approved, the creator can create batches so learners can enroll.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Take a course

A <mark style="color:green;">**Learner**</mark> discovers a course, enrolls in a batch, works through lessons and quizzes, and tracks their progress. When they finish, a certificate is issued automatically.
